# 01-IO-CIVIC — Civic, re-derived - the morale term that can never fire
> Source: `docs/Actual HTMLs Of designers/civicderived.html` · Component-designer door · Census (Phase 1 draft, unverified)

## What this door builds
This is the "civic buildings" door — the installations a colony builds to keep its people alive, housed, fed, comforted, healthy, orderly, governed, and trained. One designer produces nine distinct installation types (life-support node, residency, space habitat, farm, hospital, park, precinct, town hall, and an officer academy). The shape is **one forced choice — "what does it provide?" — that swaps in a different template with its own two or three sliders.** The academy adds a **second forced choice** (which of seven leader domains the school trains). So the door is really: pick the civic job (9 chips), then, for an academy only, pick the leader field (7 chips), then drag capacity + quality (+ automation, farm only).

The whole point of this door, in the developer's own framing, is not to design morale — it is to check whether the morale model that already exists is actually being fed. The headline finding: the `ColonyMoraleDB` employment term (a +15 / -25 forty-point band) reads `EmploymentAtbDB.Jobs`, but **no template in the game carries `EmploymentAtbDB`**, so jobs is always 0 and employment contributes exactly zero forever — a consumer with no producer. The fix the door proposes is to publish each installation's existing `CrewReq` as its `Jobs` figure.

## D. NOTES / DISCREPANCIES
- **Employment is a consumer with no producer — the door's headline (engine-pending).** `ColonyMoraleDB` has a complete two-sided employment term (`MaxEmploymentBonus=15.0`, `MaxUnemploymentPenalty=25.0`, `:132-139`), reads `GetTotalJobs` which sums `EmploymentAtbDB.Jobs`, but **no template carries `EmploymentAtbDB`**, so `employmentRatio = (jobs>0 && workforce>0)? jobs/workforce: -1.0` is permanently `-1.0` and contributes 0. **Waits on:** a producer — publish each installation's existing `CrewReq` as `EmploymentAtbDB.Jobs` (factory `Size*5`, refinery `[Mass]*0.1`, mine `Area*0.005`, lab `20`). Flagged as the biggest live behavior change of the eight doors; should be flag-gated against the `MoraleTests` baseline.
- **Space Habitat mass is a constant (PROPOSED fix, slice V1).** `space-habitat Mass = 1000 CONSTANT` — a station housing up to **1,000,000 colonists** costs one tonne / 0 credits / 100 BP, same as one housing nobody (200× its sibling's ceiling on identical cost). The `fix` checkbox previews the proposed priced formula `1000*(1 + v/C.def*0.5 + v2/10)`. **Waits on:** a design decision to price it. The habitat deliberately carries **no gravity/pressure tolerance attribute** (correct — how a station escapes a planet's environment; a documented landmine — cannot be faked by zeroing dials, which clamp to tech bounds).
- **Class Length (academy dial 2) costs TIME, not one of the seven cost channels.** `NavalAcademyAtb.cs:46 graduationDate = StarSysDateTime + TimeSpan.FromDays(TrainingPeriodInMonths*30)`. A 48-month course pays in throughput, not mass/credits. This is the counter-example to the Industrial door's "free dial" test — an honest dial that appears in no cost formula because it costs time; the door argues the test needs a justified allow-list ("it costs time" valid; "nobody noticed" not).
- **Medical (health) and Security (legitimacy) are new morale/stability consumers NOT in the six-input model.** The reconciliation table lists only Employment, Crowding, Comfort, Food, Power, Tax. Medical's "+N health" and Security's "-N unrest" (→ `LegitimacyProcessor`) are shown as effects but are **engine-pending** — no confirmed wire in `ColonyMoraleDB`. Recreation's comfort DOES feed the live comfort input (adds toward the +20 `MaxComfortBonus` cap alongside Residency).
- **Comfort dials over-range the cap.** Residency/Recreation/Habitat comfort dials run to 50 but `MaxComfortBonus=20` caps morale — the top ~60% of the range is inert (panel marks it amber `a` when `v2>MAX_COMFORT`). The reconciliation table calls the comfort dial "free and 60% of its range inert."
- **Crowding capacity dial is free.** Reconciliation notes crowding is live (population vs `PopulationSupportAtbDB`) "but the capacity dial is free" — for `infra`/`habitat` the capacity number does not always enter the mass formula (habitat especially, until `fix`).
- **Slider snap-to-default:** because the habitat's shipped 500 is 0.05% of a 1,000,000 range (unrepresentable on a 0–100 integer slider), render snaps `v` to `C.def` when `d1===STOCK[job].d1`, so the shipped design reads exactly.
