# The Acclamator, built by the playbook — a dedicated assault transport

> A REPRODUCTION build (Star Wars, Acclamator I-class). The interesting part isn't the guns — it's that the whole
> ship is a **flying garage**: it out-lands and under-guns the Venator, and the tool shows exactly that.

## Objective & role stack
Storm a contested system and put a **full clone Legion + its armour** on the ground. Stack: **① deliver the ground
force → ② break an orbital blockade (light guns) → ③ survive the drop.** Sacrifice: it's lightly armed for its size
— its tonnage is troop bays, not turbolasers.

## The honest ledger
**LIVE:** the guns, armour, shields, sensors, crew sustainment, the hyperdrive — **and the headline: it lands
368 vehicles/troops of ground force via the LIVE `GroundBayAtb → GroundTransport` invasion chain** (6× the
Venator's ground capacity). This is the one build whose *defining* capability — delivering an army — is fully
LIVE, not pending. **PENDING:** nothing load-bearing (it carries no fighters, so no fighter-launch gap).

**Verdict:** the Acclamator is the anti-Venator — where the Venator's carrier identity is stuck PENDING, the
Acclamator's assault-transport identity is **entirely LIVE**. It works, cradle to grave, today.
