Pulsar4X · the entity assembler · the machine after the 12 doors

The doors design components. The resolver reads totals. This is the machine in between.

You don't fight with a component — you fight with a whole thing: a warship, a battalion, a station. The Entity Assembler is where designed parts get mounted onto a host, spend its budgets, pass its gates, and collapse into the handful of totals the combat resolver actually reads (ShipCombatValueDB.Calculate). Pick a host, add parts, watch it come together — and watch the numbers gate.

🔑 The one thing this proves that a parts list can't: the same component behaves differently by host. A railgun's armour-penetration is live on a ground unit and inert on a ship — the two resolvers read a different subset of its profile. Switch the host below and watch the weapon table re-wire itself. (Verified in engine source, 2026-08-02.)
