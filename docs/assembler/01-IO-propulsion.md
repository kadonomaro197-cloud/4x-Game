# 01-IO-PROPULSION — Propulsion, fully derived — one door, four ways to move
> Source: `docs/Actual HTMLs Of designers/propulsionderived.html` · Component-designer door · Census (Phase 1 draft, unverified)

## What this door builds
This one door designs **every drive part** — the thing that makes a ship or a ground unit move. It answers two separate questions about a single part you are designing: **how you move *inside* a system** (sublight) and **how you *leave* one** (FTL). The forced choice at the top picks the family — **Reaction mass** (carry your own propellant), **A surface** (push against the ground), or **FTL drive** (cross between systems). Whichever family you pick, the rest of the panel is **the same two dials re-purposed** — *how much drive you fit* (drive mass) and *how you spend the budget* (a zero-sum split), plus two proposed muffling dials. The FTL family is itself **one part with three settings** (route × transit × can-you-be-found) that reproduce every science-fiction FTL as combinations. The whole shape: **1 family choice (+ up to 3 FTL sub-choices + 2 checkboxes) driving 2 always-live sliders (+ 2 proposed).**

## D. NOTES / DISCREPANCIES
- **Signature suppression (`supp`, reaction) is PROPOSED / engine-pending.** Labeled "proposed — taxes the WHOLE budget." The design ruling: `P_drive = P_total × (1 − suppression)` (pays twice — push *and* economy, plus shroud mass). Not yet a shipped sim variable; today it only reshapes the panel's own numbers.
- **Quietness (`quiet`, FTL) is PROPOSED / engine-pending.** Same "taxes the WHOLE budget." Dies (slider removed) under hidden transit and on the gate box — a hidden slider is force-zeroed (`qf=(hidden||gateOnly)?0:quiet/100`) so it "must not act on the numbers behind your back."
- **Navigator-gated (`nav`) is PROPOSED / engine-pending.** `CommandBerthAtb` exists to seat the specialist, but the wire from an FTL drive to a required berth is not built.
- **Only FTL box 1 (Star Trek warp = free·continuous·seen) is fully shipped.** Box 2 (hidden warp) needs the scan **and** the battle trigger to skip a ship in transit. Box 3 (BSG/Dune/Andromeda jump) needs a jump order + per-jump range + cooldown (spool is already emergent from creation cost). Box 4 (Stellaris lanes) is the "cheapest unbuilt box" — a rule over the existing `JumpRouter`, not a system. Box 5 (veiled lane) is an **empty box the derivation predicts, nobody claims.** Box 6 (gate coupler) — the network/discovery/router ship, but **no drive component exists**; `JumpOrder` merely checks you own a warp drive.
- **FTL now emits a signature (✅ shipped from this derivation)** — one property added to `alcubierre-warp-drive`, `inertialess-drive`, `reactionless-drive`, which previously carried none. It uses the **thruster band (600 nm ± 250)** deliberately — a proper gravimetric band (~9,660 nm) would be invisible to every receiver in the base mod (a stealth exploit). Giving FTL its own band is a flagged two-part change (signature + a matching receiver), not done.
- **Boundary dispute — `GroundLocomotionAtb` vs `NewtonionThrustAtb`.** The design says group these two "one verb, two implementations" behind this door; grouping means **deleting `GroundLocomotionAtb`** (a serialized-type removal that breaks existing saves — needs a converter or a clean-save decision).
- **Known live bug flagged by the door:** `SpeedMultForUnit` returns a mounted drive's `SpeedFactor` *outright*, so once a unit carries a designed drive its frame mode (Legs/Tracks/Walker/Hover) stops affecting speed — the four modes become dead weight. The design wants the mode to *scale* the designed factor. Behavior change; awaits a ruling + re-baselined gauge.
- **Moved OUT of this door:** the grav surveyor (`GravSurveyAtb`) → **Sensors ▸ Survey** ("finding a jump point is not moving through one"). Jump-point **stabilisation** (`IsStabilized`) is dead code (`// TODO: Introduce a random chance to stablize jumppoints.`) — flagged as a possible future player spend.
- **Collapsed/cut (would contradict a naive multi-door reading):** Reactionless/Gravitic = "reaction with a rule removed" (a flag). Inertialess = a *defensive* evasion-floor component, not propulsion. Teleport = logistics. Fluid = "reaction with the propellant scooped" (no component/variable). "Spacetime as a thing you push against" = the author's retracted error (a ship carries a thruster **and** a warp drive, so they were never alternatives). Four per-flavour FTL components collapse to **one part with three settings** (630 FTL names, 848 door-wide, no franchise IP).
- **The name is composed, never IP.** `driveName()` builds `[modifier][scale][temper][noun]` from the dials with three guards (no stutters, no contradictions, a 5-word cap that never fires). Franchise names on the page are *taxonomy analysis*, not product names.
