A planet is a board of environments

The surface is not a dimensionless dot — it's regions you own, terrain you cross, and weather that rolls through, all sitting under one planetary condition (its gravity, air, heat, radiation, and daylight). Where those layers stack is the ground a battle is fought on — and that stack is the setting the auto-resolver reads. Pick a world, click a hex, and read the fight it produces.

→ How the ground becomes the fight

This is the load-bearing connection. You don't pick a battle's environment from a menu — the location picks it for you. Terrain (from the hex), condition (from the planet), and weather (rolling through) compose into one environment, and the auto-resolver opens the fight with exactly that. Your only environment decision is where you commit.

Two settings meet on the field. The environment is the ground's call — fixed by where you fight (this map). Your doctrine — stance (close / hold / stand-off / withdraw) and target priority — is your call, set in the resolver. A battle is the region's environment ✕ your doctrine, run through the one shared kernel. The planet view is the front door; the resolver is the room.
