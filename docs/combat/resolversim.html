What this is, and how faithful it is

This is the LD-30 arena model (AUTO-RESOLVER-GROUND-TRUTH §5): the two sides open at opposite ends of a diameter of an arena bounded by the longest weapon range, the circle does not shrink, and the fight is resolved by the units moving at their real speeds. Every damage formula and tuning number is copied from the engine's real kernel and cited to its file & line (see the Constants tab); the kernel cross-check runs on load. Weapon ranges are engine-true; the one thing the engine doesn't expose as a spec-sheet field — a ship's closing speed — is an editable per-class stand-in (flagged in Honesty). Damage, toughness and shields are in MJ (joules ÷ 1,000,000; the kernel is linear so this changes no outcome). Companion dossier: docs/combat/RESOLVER-SIM.md.
