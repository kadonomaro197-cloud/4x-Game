Deterministic by construction — the engine's damage math has no RNG and no clock, so Resolve (fast-forward) and Play (watched) reach the identical result, and Replay re-runs a scenario bit-for-bit. Seed / iteration order are fixed.
What this is, and how faithful it is

Every formula and tuning number below is copied from the engine source and cited to its file & line (see the Constants tab). Damage, toughness and shields are shown in MJ (the engine's joules ÷ 1,000,000; the kernel is linear so this changes no outcome). Velocities are m/s and ranges metres, exactly as the engine reads them. Where the engine has a real bug or a deliberate gap, the sim reproduces the engine-true result and flags it — it never quietly "fixes" the engine. Companion dossier: docs/combat/RESOLVER-SIM.md.
