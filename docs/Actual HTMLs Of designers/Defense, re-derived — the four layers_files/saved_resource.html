Defense, re-derived — the four layers

Pulsar4X · component designer · door 2 of 11

Defense is an onion, and the resolver already peels it in order.

Miss → shield → armour → structure. Four layers, four different mechanics, four different counters — and only one of them is even sold at the Defense door today.

The four layers

Derived from what the resolver reads off a defended target. This is its real order of operations.

1  Evasionyou miss entirely

Beaten by shot speed, tracking or sheer volume. Does not care about shot size. — and it is not a Defense component: it comes from hull volume and engine acceleration.

2  Shielda recharging buffer

Soaks a fraction of what arrives and depletes. Beaten by sustained fire and by Exotic. The only layer that comes back on its own. Ignores shot size completely.

3  Armoura flat bounce, per shot

Subtracts a flat amount from every single shot, floored at 10%. Beaten by big shots and by penetration. Never recharges. Shot size is everything here.

4  Structureraw hit points

No cleverness — it absorbs until it runs out. Beaten by nothing in particular; it just ends.

The mirror

This is why Defense was worth deriving second — it confirms the weapons derivation from the other side.

Armourthe DEFENDER — plate thickness and its nature tuningyes
Structurenobodyno

You cannot build an anti-energy shield. A shield’s response to a damage type is fixed by what is being fired at it — the defender gets no say. Armour is the opposite: its four nature resistances are entirely the defender’s choice. That asymmetry is the same one the weapons page states from the attacking side, and it holds.

What the four authored doors got wrong

Hardening is not a combat defence

It writes EnvironmentalResistance, and that value is read in one place: the environmental attrition step (GroundForcesProcessor.cs:235). It never touches a weapon hit. It belongs with survival and environment, not beside armour and shields.

Fortification is not something you wear

It is a building that modifies a place — a divisor on incoming, capped at halving it. Different mechanic, different owner, ground only. It is infrastructure, not kit.

Evasion — the best defence in the game — is not sold here

It comes from hull volume and engine acceleration. To buy evasion you buy a smaller chassis or a bigger drive. The only component that overrides it is filed under Propulsion. The Defense door cannot sell you the layer that stops damage from ever being rolled.

“Armour” is two different mechanics wearing one name

On a ship, armour folds into Toughness as a lump of hit points, plus an optional nature fraction where 0 means plain. On the ground, armour is a flat per-shot subtraction with a nature multiplier where 1.0 means plain. Opposite defaults, different maths. The flat-bounce identity the kernel is built around only actually happens on the ground — the space path zeroes per-shot energy, so plate never bites there.

What this means for the doors

Two real combat layers belong at Defense: Shield (capacity ↔ regen) and Armour (thickness + nature tuning). Structure comes from the chassis; evasion comes from chassis and drive.

Hardening moves out to environment/survival. Fortification moves out to infrastructure.

🔒 RULED 2026-07-29: tuning is zero-sum — “tuning against one costs you against the others, and if you want better defense you fit more.” The four values always sum to 4.0. So there are two separate axes: how much plate raises total protection and is paid in mass; how it is tuned only ever redistributes, and is free.

Why that is the right call: tuning becomes a bet rather than an upgrade — a wager on what the enemy fields, which they can counter. It also makes intel load-bearing: knowing what a rival shoots is what tells you how to tune, wiring Defense to the sensor and espionage layers for free. And it keeps the axes honest — tougher against everything costs mass; tougher against one thing for free is impossible, you can only choose where to be weak.
