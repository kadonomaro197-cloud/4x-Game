Defense is an onion, and the resolver already peels it in the same order every time.

A shot meets four layers, in this order: Evasion → Shield → Armour → Structure. Four different mechanics, four different counters — and the Defense door builds only the middle two. The outer skin (evasion) and the core (structure) are set somewhere else, and this door can only read them. So the whole door is really one forced choice — shield or plate — and the dials that shape it.

🔑 The load-bearing thing this door proves is an ASYMMETRY. A shield's strength against a hit is decided by the attacker — the nature of the weapon fixes the soak fraction (Kinetic 100% / Energy 50% / Explosive 75% / Exotic 0%), identical on every target in the galaxy, and you the defender get no say. Armour is the mirror: its four nature-resists are entirely the defender's choice, and there is never a single published "energy-vs-armour" number, because the plate carries its own. Against shields the attacker decides; against armour the defender decides; against hull nobody does.

effective health = Health × evasion-mult × armour-mult, with the shield the one depleting pool laid on top. Evasion is the strongest term of the three — an effective-health multiplier up to ×20, where armour reaches ×10 — which is exactly why it is not sold here: you buy it with a smaller chassis or a bigger drive, not a Defense component.

The four layers, in the order the shot meets them

Derived from what the shared combat kernel reads off a defended target (Combat/CombatKernel.cs). Two are this door's to build; two are read from elsewhere.

1  Evasionread — chassis + propulsion

Is a shot even rolled at all. An effective-health multiplier up to ×20, beaten by shot speed, tracking, or sheer volume of fire. Does not care about shot size. It comes from hull volume (a smaller chassis is harder to hit) and thrust-to-mass (a livelier drive dodges) — so the Defense door reads it and cannot sell it.

2  ShieldDOOR — Capacity + Regen

A depleting pool taken before HP. It soaks a fraction of what arrives — and that fraction is set by the attacker's weapon nature, not by you. Beaten by sustained fire and by Exotic (which it cannot touch at all). The only layer that comes back on its own. Ignores shot size completely.

3  ArmourDOOR — Thickness + 4 zero-sum resists

A flat subtraction from every single shot (ArmourSoakPerPoint 1.5, floored so at least 10% always lands). Never recharges. Shot size is everything — a spray of small volleys is bounced; one big alpha punches through. The plate carries its own four nature-resists, so ablative laminate shrugs energy and goes thin vs slugs.

4  Structureread — chassis

Raw hit points. No cleverness — it absorbs until it runs out, beaten by nothing in particular. It comes from the Chassis door's frame size, so the Defense door reads it too.

What the four authored doors got wrong — and what moved

Hardening is not a combat defence — it moved to environment/survival

It writes EnvironmentalResistance, read in one place: the environmental attrition step (GroundForcesProcessor.cs). It never touches a weapon hit — it decides whether a unit survives vacuum, toxins, or radiation. It belongs with survival and the environment, not beside armour and shields. (It is now Defense ▸ Hardening 5.3 → ground attrition.)

Fortification is not something you wear — it is a place you build

A building that modifies a location: a divisor on incoming, capped at halving it, ground only. Different mechanic, different owner. It is infrastructure, not kit.

Evasion — the best defence in the game — is not sold here

It comes from hull volume and engine acceleration. To buy evasion you buy a smaller chassis or a bigger drive; the only component that overrides it is filed under Propulsion. The Defense door cannot sell you the layer that stops damage from ever being rolled — it can only read the multiplier the chassis and drive already produced.

Where the door stands

Two real combat layers belong at Defense: Shield (Capacity ↔ Regen) and Armour (Thickness + four zero-sum nature resists). Structure comes from the chassis; evasion from chassis and drive — both read, neither sold here.

🔒 RULED 2026-07-29: tuning is zero-sum — the four resists always sum to 4.0. Two separate axes: how much plate raises total protection and is paid in mass; how it is tuned only redistributes, and is free. So tuning is a bet on what the enemy fields, which makes intel load-bearing — knowing what a rival shoots is what tells you how to tune, wiring Defense to the sensor and espionage layers for free.

The asymmetry is the keystone and it is symmetric across the door and the weapons door: vs shields the attacker decides (fixed by nature, published once for the galaxy); vs armour the defender decides (four resists, never a single published number); vs hull nobody decides.
